# cybertron_5 group
project - freshguard
agriculture . food safety . sustainable nutrition
solution statement-
FreshGuard is an interactive web-based solution that addresses post-harvest food loss by helping consumers track food expiry in real time. By enabling users to monitor shelf life through visual safety indicators and engaging micro-interactions, the platform reduces avoidable food waste at the consumption stage of the agricultural value chain. This directly supports agricultural sustainability by lowering demand-side losses, improves food safety by preventing consumption of expired food, and promotes sustainable nutrition through responsible and timely food usage
“Agriculture doesn’t end at harvest — reducing food loss at the consumer level improves overall agricultural efficiency and sustainability.”
